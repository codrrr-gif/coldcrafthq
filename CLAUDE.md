## Cold Outbound
For ANY cold-outbound task — cold email, list building, segmentation,
enrichment, signal/offer decisions, or "why isn't this converting" —
invoke the `cold-outbound` skill FIRST and follow its read-order before
using coldiq-gtm, elite-copywriting, or other execution skills.

`go-to-market` decides *which* motion to run and whether outbound is the
right play; once outbound is the chosen motion, `cold-outbound` is the front
door that governs how it executes. Hand back up to `go-to-market` only for
motion choice, channel mix, beachhead, or pipeline-math questions.
