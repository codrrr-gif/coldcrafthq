# The Condensed Pre-Flight

**The lightweight gate for known ground. A companion to the Cold Outbound Constitution.**

The full Pre-Flight (Constitution, Article V) is a **discovery instrument** — it forces you to prove every causal layer before writing *when you don't yet know the answers.* This condensed version is for the opposite condition: when the upstream is already settled and stable, and re-deriving it from scratch would be ceremony with no discovery in it.

The deciding variable is your **epistemic state — what you actually *know* versus what you *assume.***

---

## When to use which

```
HIGH uncertainty  → FULL PRE-FLIGHT (Article V)
  • New vertical or subvertical
  • New or unvalidated offer
  • New market, or after a market shift
  • Anything you're guessing at
  → You must prove each layer, because guessing is expensive.

LOW uncertainty   → CONDENSED PRE-FLIGHT (this page)
  • Established subvertical you understand cold
  • Proven, calibrated offer
  • Known sophistication level and working signals
  • Just launching another play within known ground
  → The heavy layers are already known; check only what's volatile.
```

---

## The assumption this gate rests on

The condensed path is valid **only if** Layers 1–3 and a proven Layer 5 are genuinely known from prior, validated work:

- **Layer 1 — Segment:** you already know exactly who and who isn't.
- **Layer 2 — Positioning:** fixed and proven in this segment.
- **Layer 3 — Sophistication:** graded, and you know the altitude that lands.
- **Layer 5 — Offer:** proven and calibrated in recent campaigns.

If any of these is actually an *assumption* rather than *knowledge*, stop — you are in high uncertainty. Run the full Pre-Flight.

---

## The condensed check (volatile layers only)

Even on known ground, four things move between campaigns. Check exactly these:

```
[ ] SIGNAL still live    — Layer 4. Is the trigger current? Graded correctly
                           THIS time (H / M / L)? Claim matched to its weight?

[ ] OFFER still calibrated — Layer 5. Is it still priced for the trust that
                           exists right now? (Trust conditions drift.)

[ ] MESSAGE accurate      — Layers 6–8. Is THIS specific message accurate to
                           THIS specific moment — not just reused on autopilot?

[ ] COUNCIL unanimous     — Article III. Never skipped. Cheap insurance against
                           shipping something dead.
   First-Principles · Contrarian · Expansionist · Outsider · Executor
```

All four pass → cleared to write. Invoke the craft skills (`elite-copywriting`, `coldiq-gtm`) for delivery.

---

## The guardrail

> **The condensed path is a privilege earned by prior rigor — not a license to skip it.**

The failure mode is using this page because you're *lazy* rather than because you genuinely *know.* Two rules keep it honest:

1. **It is only valid while the assumptions hold.** The moment Layers 1–3 or 5 become uncertain again — new segment, new offer, new competitor, changed market — you are back in discovery. Run the full Pre-Flight.

2. **A metric drop kicks you back to full.** Per the Sharpening Loop (Article IX): *a meaningful drop in a previously-stable metric (volume unchanged) means an assumption you were treating as known has quietly stopped being true.* That is a signal to **re-run the full Pre-Flight**, not to tweak one line. The condensed gate assumes stability; a drop is evidence the stability is gone.
